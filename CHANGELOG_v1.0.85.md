# LabOS REV 1.0.85 — visible physical-stock receiving

The Material Availability resolver now contains a prominent **Receive physical lab stock** action directly below its root-cause message. It opens the controlled receipt form for the exact BOM lines, records lot/batch, quantity, supplier/source and controlled reference, then adds the stock to inventory and reserves it to the build automatically.

The standalone inventory register remains available in **Lab Standards & Resources → Lab material stock**.
