## Primary root cause

The power-tool demo-data scheduler added in REV 1.0.79 and still present in REV 1.0.80 did not use the production planning engine. It:

1. selected the first equipment matching the required capability;
2. selected the first person with the required skill;
3. advanced the next activity by a calendar-day helper; and
4. wrote the booking directly with `estimateBasis: 'Seeded power-tool demo plan'`.

It did **not** validate the chosen future slot against calibration expiry, maintenance due date, staff qualification validity, already-booked equipment/staff capacity, or planning events.

The REV 1.0.80 source even declared `usedEq` and `usedSt` maps but never used either map to prevent collisions. Therefore the schedule looked planned in the swimlane while bypassing the same constraints the readiness service later enforced.

## Portfolio-level proof from the unmodified REV 1.0.80 demo

A runtime audit of the REV 1.0.80 generated demo state found:

- **34 equipment-readiness failures**;
- **17 equipment double-booking overlaps**;
- **121 staff double-booking overlaps**;
- **0 staff qualification failures** in that seed;
- P26-1024 accounted for the exact **4 equipment-readiness failures** shown in the screenshot.

All 34 equipment-readiness failures came from only two resources:

- **EQ-011:** 21 failed future bookings;
- **EQ-007:** 13 failed future bookings.

This proves the blocker checker was correctly exposing invalid bookings created upstream by the shortcut seed scheduler.

## Secondary planner defect found during root-cause testing

While testing hard lab closures, a second boundary defect was found. AUTO-PLAN checked equipment readiness at its initial cursor, then capacity search could push the final slot later — potentially across a calibration or maintenance due date. The final shifted slot was not revalidated before booking.

REV 1.0.81 now performs a mandatory readiness recheck at the actual final candidate slot. If capacity movement crosses a due date, required care is scheduled/reused and the task is searched again after that care. The booking is not accepted until both equipment and staff are valid at the actual start time.

## REV 1.0.81 corrections and acceptance result

REV 1.0.81:

- removes the shortcut demo scheduler;
- seeds the demo with the production `PlannerService`;
- migrates existing schema-30 / REV 1.0.80 demo data and atomically replans the legacy seed bookings;
- keeps whole-lab closures and other planning events as hard constraints during migration;
- preserves current commitments for review;
- removes obsolete future seed bookings from Released / Delivered / Closed demo builds;
- preserves completed/actual booking history during replanning;
- reuses already scheduled calibration/maintenance instead of duplicating it;
- rechecks readiness at the final capacity-resolved slot;
- groups equipment-blocker UI by root resource and shows explicit calibration/maintenance causes.

Final verification: **30/30 automated checks passed**. The fresh REV 1.0.81 demo returns zero equipment-readiness failures, zero staff-qualification failures, zero equipment overlaps, zero staff overlaps, zero hard planning-event overlaps, and zero equipment-capability mismatches. The exact four-failure P26-1024 fixture repairs to zero, while a whole-lab closure remains preserved.
