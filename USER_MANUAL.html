LABOS · REV 1.0.47

User Manual

Task guidance for prototype builds. Primary screens show the action that matters now; secondary rationale and evidence are kept in foldouts.

1 · Customer & request

Add customers: Administrator → Configuration & data → Customers & requirements. Customer-specific retention and approvals are then available to requests.

Find work: Prototype Requests supports customer/product/project filters and sorting by submitted date, required date, product, customer or priority.

2 · Guided build workflow

Open a build and follow the numbered workflow. Green ✓ = satisfied, yellow ● = current/open, red ✕ = blocked. A blocker must lead to its exact corrective action rather than a generic dashboard.

At each process step: choose samples → start execution → capture machine/setup and sample/Control Plan data → complete execution. Sample data can be bulk-filled, edited per sample, or round-tripped by CSV.

3 · Control Plan and external risk analysis

LabOS owns the Control Plan and its objective evidence. It does not duplicate the organisation's risk-analysis system. Applicable special/safety characteristics and controls from external risk analysis are flowed into the Control Plan, then into the route-step sample matrix.

4 · Calibration, maintenance & training

Open Resource Assurance. Use Schedule calibration, Schedule maintenance or Schedule training for a new reservation, or schedule forecast due items from the board. The selected slot reserves the relevant equipment/person and the planner respects it.

Equipment Timing sets calibration and maintenance duration. Competency definitions set training duration. Conflicts are shown before save; an explicitly accepted conflict flags the affected build for replan.

Completion evidence

Calibration stores the certificate; maintenance stores the work order/certificate/evidence file; training stores the competency certificate. Readiness reports provide links to the stored documents or controlled URLs.

5 · Audit Readiness

Define organisation + site/laboratory + scope statement before generating an audit pack. Empty scope is not accepted.

Every Major and Minor finding has Resolve →. The dialog shows only the affected records, required correction, evidence to retain and automatic recheck step. Records-retention and internal-audit references are maintained with the audit scope so these findings also have a direct closure path.

Recommended audit evidence sets

Audit scope/boundaries; laboratory capability & uncertainty scope; calibration register/certificates; maintenance register/evidence; competency/training matrix/certificates; controlled methods/work instructions; Control Plan/special-characteristic register; sample genealogy/traceability; quality/nonconformity/corrective-action register; approved build/release dossiers; audit/change trail; resource-assurance schedule; and an optional management KPI snapshot.

6 · Reports & printing

Resource Assurance provides calibration, maintenance and training reports plus printable combined or activity-specific schedules. Build Reports consolidate route execution, Control Plan evidence, sample data, test/characterisation, genealogy, quality records and approvals.

7 · Improvement actions

If LabOS can safely implement an accepted proposal itself, it updates the controlled plan/master data and recalculates impact. If the action is physical or governed—such as purchasing equipment or qualifying a person—the accepted proposal opens a guided implementation workflow and stays open until effectiveness is verified.

Production deployment boundary

This static POC is not a production QMS/LIMS. Enterprise deployment needs authenticated users, server-side permissions, shared transactional storage, electronic-signature controls where applicable, immutable audit retention, backups/security validation and integrations to corporate systems.
