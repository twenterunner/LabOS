LABOS · REV 1.0.126

User Manual

Task guidance for prototype builds. The normal screen shows the current action; supporting rationale and already-satisfied evidence are kept in foldouts.

REV 1.0.126 · KPI REPORTING PERIOD

One selected period drives the KPI page

Open Lab Performance and choose the reporting window at the top. Delivery, quality, readiness, capacity/utilization, cost, 5S, replan causes and Internal network utilization use that same selected period. Choose the last 13 weeks, last 12 months, last 8 quarters, one week, one month or a custom From/To range.

The Business-unit allocation card uses the active lab's bookings inside the selected period. Switch the pie between Time spent and Direct resource cost. Business unit comes from Configuration → Project Teams; direct resource cost uses booking time multiplied by the controlled labour-role and equipment rates.

REV 1.0.126 · REPLAN REASONS & HISTORY

Every replan has an operational reason

When you manually move a task in the swimlane, both Green and Yellow options now ask for a reason category and a free-text explanation before anything is applied. The full Manual Planner requires the same information before Save. Accepted AUTO-PLAN / portfolio replans keep their mandatory planning-decision note and also capture the reason category.

Task-level replan history is retained separately from delivery-commitment history, so a resource/day change remains measurable even when the promised delivery date does not change. Open the build Planning step to see Task Replan History. The KPI tab shows Replan causes for the selected period, and the Commitment & replan history CSV exports both task and commitment events.

REV 1.0.126 · MANUAL REPLAN vs AUTO PLAN

A reason is not automatically a permanent lock

When you manually move a task, the selected date becomes the live schedule and your reason stays in the audit/replan history. By default the move is re-optimizable: a later AUTO PLAN may propose moving the task again if it finds a better complete plan without worsening a critical delivery KPI.

Use Protect this slot from AUTO PLAN only when the manually selected date/resource is a true hard operational commitment. Protected slots are retained by the optimizer. Unprotected manual delays can be shown in AUTO PLAN as a specific refinement, including the current date, proposed date and your original reason. AUTO PLAN never silently reverses the decision; you still review and accept/reject the proposal.

REV 1.0.126 · MANUAL MOVE TRANSACTION

Validated moves are committed by stable task identity

The unified planner may regenerate internal booking IDs when it rebuilds dependent work. REV 1.0.126 therefore verifies a manual move using the stable build + canonical task identity and the prevalidated date/equipment/person target instead of expecting the old booking ID to survive. Stale plans are still rejected through the live-plan fingerprint and final integrity checks.

REV 1.0.124 · STABILITY & NETWORK ROUTING

Routed work stays visible and stale routing is fail-safe

A single process/test/development/closeout operation routed to a sister lab remains part of the home build timeline and is also visible as incoming work when the receiving lab is selected. This is a display scope only; the planning engine still enforces independent lab resource pools.

If a selected sister lab later becomes inactive, AUTO PLAN refuses to schedule there. Use the guided recovery to choose another active sister lab or return only that operation to the build lab. If the underlying task definition is removed, LabOS removes its obsolete site override and manual constraint and marks the previous accepted transfer as superseded while retaining the history.

REV 1.0.124 · EXTERNAL COMMERCIAL EVIDENCE

Only current, relevant evidence drives sourcing economics

Expired or not-yet-valid test quotes remain traceable but are not used as the active external benchmark. LabOS falls back to the generic external-facility cost until a valid controlled quote exists.

Changing supplier, price, pricing basis, fees, lead time, reference or validity creates a superseded commercial history record. Any old attachment stays with that old record and is detached from the new terms unless a replacement proof is uploaded. This prevents an old quote or invoice from appearing to prove a new price.

REV 1.0.123 · EXTERNAL TEST COST & EVIDENCE

Maintain the real outsourcing benchmark for each Standard Test

Open Lab Standards & Resources → Standard Test Library and select Edit standard & external cost on the required test. Enable the test-specific benchmark, then enter the external supplier/test house, pricing basis (fixed per test, per sample or per batch), price, setup/admin cost, transport/handling, nominal lead time, quote/invoice reference and validity dates.

Use Attach quote / invoice proof to retain the supporting PDF, image, Office document or text file with the controlled test record. LabOS retains the filename, file type/size, uploader and upload time and provides an Open / download evidence action. In this browser POC the attachment is stored locally with application data and is limited to 8 MB; production should use governed document/object storage.

When a Standard Test has a specific external price, sister-lab and external-recovery comparisons use that commercial value before the generic external-facility hourly rate. An accepted single-step sister-lab transfer records the external spend avoided and the supporting commercial reference. Expired evidence remains traceable but is flagged for refresh.

REV 1.0.123 · SINGLE-STEP SISTER-LAB ROUTING

Route any active planned step without moving the build

In Planning or a build-specific Schedule swimlane, tap any future canonical planned step and choose Route only this step to a sister lab. This applies to process steps, formal tests, method/process-development bookings and the final quality review / release / handover booking. LabOS evaluates each active internal sister lab with the canonical planner, keeps build ownership unchanged, applies the appropriate site-transfer lead time, replans dependent work and rejects any candidate that fails task coverage or whole-portfolio integrity.

For the final quality/release/handover booking, routing changes the planned execution location only; it does not delegate or bypass the controlled approval roles. Completed/historical bookings remain locked. Planning-situation warning rows are diagnostic markers rather than executable work and therefore are not transferable.

REV 1.0.120 · IN-SWIMLANE MANUAL PLANNING

Green and Yellow alternatives are shown directly across the timeline

Tap a planned task and choose Move / replan in swimlane. LabOS checks the complete visible calendar width with the same canonical day-level solver used by AUTO PLAN. Every end-to-end feasible day is painted directly in the selected task lane: Green means current released capability/readiness is sufficient and no other build moves; Yellow means the day becomes feasible after a controlled calibration, maintenance or training action. Exact clock times remain internal solver details.

The same behavior is available in Planning and in the specific Build workspace, including the sticky build-plan swimlane. If the next valid day lies outside the current view, LabOS expands the date window to the next feasible solution and rescans the full displayed width. Structural equipment or skill gaps open guided recovery instead of ending with a dead message. A normal task tap still opens the Planned Task dialog; only the explicit move action or desktop move grip enters replanning mode.

REV 1.0.118 · MOBILE PLANNING

Tap a task to inspect it; moving is always explicit

On phones and tablets, tapping any planned swimlane bar opens the Planned Task dialog. Finger movement while tapping can no longer start manual replanning. For a test, the dialog includes Send only this test to a sister lab; the rest of the build remains at its current lab. Use Move / replan this step when you intentionally want to change the day.

On desktop, the small move grip remains available for intentional drag interaction. The booking bar itself is never draggable.

REV 1.0.118 · MANUAL PLANNING

Choose the day; LabOS assigns the exact working time

Tap a future process/test in a swimlane and choose Move / replan this step. Manual planning now shows calendar-day alternatives only. The unified planning engine assigns the exact start/finish time, qualified person and capable equipment internally, and validates all downstream work before an option is shown.

The visible swimlane window is not the search horizon. LabOS searches beyond it for the next complete feasible day, including weeks or months later. Green uses current controlled readiness. Yellow inserts validated training/calibration/maintenance first. If no registered equipment capability or controlled skill path exists, use the guided resolution offered by LabOS.

REV 1.0.115 · MULTI-LAB KPI SCOPE

Planning metrics follow the selected execution laboratory

The six Master Planner health tiles and the Build Commitment Health list are now calculated strictly from builds whose executionSiteId belongs to the selected Active lab. Switching Twente, Stuttgart or Detroit therefore changes All, On time, Late builds, Total days late, Commitment movement and Unplanned to that laboratory only. A controlled sister-lab transfer moves the build into the receiving lab's operational metrics while home-site history remains retained.

The post-render planning layer also re-enters the active-lab scope before rebuilding filters and grouped timeline headers, preventing enterprise-wide data from leaking back into a lab-local planning screen.

REV 1.0.109 · PLANNING SAFETY

LIVE baseline, shared readiness and fault-safe transfer

Sister-lab transfers: calibration, maintenance and qualification/readiness reservations created by AUTO PLAN belong to the controlled resource/portfolio. Moving or replanning the build that originally triggered them no longer deletes readiness needed by other accepted work. Every sister-lab candidate and final transfer is checked against the entire portfolio before it can be accepted.

Scenario Lab: the comparison table now separates Current LIVE baseline, Scenario as-is and verified optimization options. LIVE is the optimization reference. The Scenario as-is row is the simulated disruption before recovery and is never a recommendation. An optimization must be conflict-free, must not move another accepted project later or worsen critical delivery outcomes versus LIVE, and must improve at least one outcome.

Scenario application also refuses to apply a stale analysis after LIVE planning changed and repeats whole-portfolio integrity validation before commit.

REV 1.0.109 · INPUT & RESPONSIVE BOUNDARIES

Reject invalid demand instead of normalising it

Required dates are validated as real calendar dates, so values such as 30 February are rejected rather than rolled into March. Request quantity must be a positive whole number and defaults to a maximum of 5,000 per controlled build. Sample generation also requires a non-negative whole number and cannot exceed the request/material limit.

Request filters and Scenario Lab controls now reflow/contain themselves at compact Android widths and standard desktop breakpoints.

REV 1.0.108 · ANDROID / COMPACT SCREENS

Switching demo roles on mobile

On Android, tablets and compact browser windows, tap the hamburger menu. The Demo role selector is now shown at the top of the navigation drawer. Choose the required role; LabOS saves the selection, refreshes the role-based navigation and dashboard, and closes the drawer automatically.

The role selector is deliberately kept out of the crowded compact top bar. On full desktop widths the original header role selector remains available. Both selectors stay synchronized.

REV 1.0.108 · SCENARIO INPUTS

Only ask for assumptions that change the simulation

Current risk / recovery scan uses the current LIVE portfolio and therefore asks for no dates or resource. Priority escalation also needs no dates. Material delay asks only when material will be available. Equipment outage and person absence ask for the affected resource plus an unavailable date range; lab closure asks only for the unavailable date range.

Hidden fields are not stored as scenario assumptions. This prevents inactive default dates from appearing to constrain a recovery scan.

REV 1.0.108 · SCENARIO LAB STAGE 2

Optimization & recovery on an operational twin

Open Planning → Scenario Lab. Select a target build and optionally simulate an equipment outage, person absence, lab closure, priority escalation or material delay. The scenario is clearly marked NOT LIVE; it runs on a disposable copy of the current operational state.

In REV 1.0.109, the untouched Current LIVE baseline is the optimization reference; Scenario as-is separately shows the selected assumption applied before recovery. Generate & rank better options runs the same canonical constrained-planning engine as LIVE planning and tests protected resequencing, alternative people/equipment, controlled readiness/training, target-first escalation, portfolio trade-offs, weekend capacity, complete sister-lab routing, external facilities and governed split-route concepts.

LabOS suppresses alternatives that do not produce a verified non-worsening improvement versus the current LIVE baseline. Results with an unresolved target or residual scenario conflicts are not proposed. The comparison table shows the baseline beside every surviving option with target finish/status, network OTD, total portfolio late days, remaining conflicts, worsened projects, moved bookings, added cost and a concise improvement-vs-baseline explanation.

If no option beats the baseline, LabOS explicitly says to keep the baseline instead of manufacturing a recommendation. If the baseline contains a structural scenario conflict and no verified recovery is better, change the assumptions or resolve the blocker.

Use Review impact before applying. Apply to LIVE requires a rationale, creates audit evidence, rejects non-improving options and retains one-click undo. A split-route result remains a review proposal only; transport/custody/handover feasibility must be governed before mixed-site execution is planned.

Use Save analysis to retain the compact scenario definition and result summary. Running it again solves against the latest LIVE state rather than reusing stale candidate schedules.

REV 1.0.108 · AUTO PLAN DECISION LOGIC

Feasible does not automatically mean better

When AUTO PLAN runs, the current accepted LIVE plan is the baseline. A result is shown under Verified optimization options only when it is no worse than baseline for unplanned builds, total days late, maximum project delay, commitment worsening and priority-weighted lateness, and at least one outcome improves.

The comparison table places the LIVE baseline beside every verified optimization before you choose. A plan that schedules more previously-unplanned work but creates additional lateness is not called an optimization. If it is complete and potentially useful for blocker recovery, it appears separately under Recovery trade-offs — not optimization proposals, with a clear What improves / What gets worse summary. Such an option can be reviewed deliberately but is never marked Recommended.

If one recovery result is equal or worse than another recovery result on all compared KPIs, LabOS suppresses the inferior result. If no tested strategy safely beats the baseline, AUTO PLAN explicitly tells you to keep the current baseline. Partial candidates that still contain blockers remain in the guided recovery workflow instead of being presented as plans to accept.

REV 1.0.102 · PLANNING & LAB NETWORK

Day-level planning and unambiguous network routing

Planning timelines now show day-level resolution. Swipe left/right on touch devices or click-drag the chart with a mouse to move the visible date window; use −, Fit and + to change zoom. Exact working-hour timestamps remain internal so equipment, people, readiness and dependency conflicts are still validated.

Lab Network Recovery contains only active builds that have no canonical plan, an incomplete/unresolved canonical plan, or a complete plan that finishes late. A stale forecast field is never shown as a reliable finish when plan coverage is incomplete.

Compare sister labs distinguishes the current accepted plan finish from a fresh replan from now. A current reserved plan may therefore finish earlier than a new replan against today’s remaining capacity. Any active project can also be routed electively to a sister lab; Recovery and Elective moves are tracked separately in management KPI.

REV 1.0.100 · LAB NETWORK

Independent laboratories, one planning engine

Use the Active lab selector to work inside one laboratory. People, equipment, material stock, closures/readiness events and operational planning are owned by that lab. Products carry separate default sites for Prototype, Validation / Testing and Failure Analysis; new requests route to the corresponding default while existing controlled builds never move silently.

When home-lab timing is late or unresolved, open Planning → Lab Network Recovery → Compare sister labs. LabOS runs the same canonical planner independently at each internal site, shows the real forecast and requested-date result, and compares those alternatives with the configured external-facility cost/lead-time benchmark. A sister-site result becomes live only after Review transfer, rationale and acceptance. The original home site remains traceable. Network KPIs then report sister-lab builds, internal hours retained and estimated outsourcing spend avoided.

Administrators configure sites and product defaults in Laboratory network & product routing. Stable site IDs are intentionally suitable for later ERP/PLM/API/SSO/SCIM integration.

REV 1.0.100 · PLANNING

One engine, one timeline, one deadline definition

AUTO PLAN, MANUAL PLAN and Escalation share the same controlled scheduling engine and canonical build-task definition. In every planning swimlane use − · Fit · + for zoom and swipe/click-drag horizontally to move the visible date window earlier or later. REV 1.0.90 · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE & VISUAL CONSISTENCY

LabOS now separates controlled definitions, operational assurance, planning scenarios, configuration and management reporting more explicitly. REV 1.0.59 · GAGE R&R

System recommendation, human decision

Quality → MSA / Gage R&R shows a conservative automotive recommendation separately from the authorised reviewer decision. The default guide uses a balanced crossed study, typically 10 parts, 2–3 operators and 2–3 repeated trials, <10% as acceptable, 10–30% as application-dependent, >30% as unacceptable, and ndc ≥5. IATF 16949 does not create one universal numeric acceptance table; customer-specific requirements and intended-use risk take precedence. Any decision that overrides the LabOS recommendation requires retained rationale.

Scheduling alone never clears readiness.

REV 1.0.59 · SIGN-OFFS

CORE UX CONTRACT

No dead ends

An actionable recommendation must either be implemented by LabOS when accepted or lead through a functional guided workflow to resolution, evidence and recalculation. Partial capacity changes and accepted-but-unimplemented improvement proposals are not valid states.

Planning situations

A staff absence, equipment outage or lab closure is simulated together with the resulting portfolio plan. If the complete solution is blocked, the exact blocker opens its competency/equipment/staff resolver. The pending proposal retries automatically after resolution. The constraint and schedule are accepted together or neither is saved.

RESOURCE ASSURANCE

Calibration, maintenance and training

IMPROVEMENT

Accept means implement

The Improvement Scan only surfaces prevalidated staffing/equipment load-balancing proposals that can be applied completely. Acceptance rechecks qualification/readiness/conflicts, changes the actual controlled bookings/route assignments and verifies the result before committing.

Production deployment boundary

This static POC is not a production QMS/LIMS. Enterprise deployment needs authenticated users, server-side permissions, shared transactional storage, validated e-signatures where applicable, immutable audit retention, backups/security validation and governed integrations to corporate systems.

LESSONS & LEARNING

Evidence-based closeout learning

At Engineering handover & close, LabOS derives candidate lessons from the build record: quality/yield exceptions, schedule churn, material constraints, process-time overruns, failed controlled measurements and released method development. Duplicate evidence is consolidated. Every proposal must be accepted or rejected with rationale before closeout. Accepted learning is retained and included in the Build Report.

REV 1.0.115 planning and route-review behavior

In Planning → Per build, the red required-delivery marker spans each task lane for the selected build. Tap a future task and choose Move / replan this step to see validated Green, Yellow and Red alternatives. Green is the least-disruptive feasible choice. Completed/historical work cannot be moved.
